PRISMEMBER - The unicorn's seventh light

STORY
The world has lost its colour. Grey fell from the sky like a very soft snow,
and everything turned the same shade. You are the last unicorn - and your horn
is a prism. It can refract the colours you still remember back into the world.

Seven chapters, seven colours: red like a heartbeat, orange like dusk, yellow
like a window that stayed lit at night, green like a hand smaller than your
mane, cyan like rain, blue like an eye, violet like yourself. Walk the grey
hills, find each closed bud, choose the colour it is missing, and shine your
horn at it until it opens.

But light is not free. Every ray you spend is a memory dissolving: the line of
your own story fades letter by letter as you play, and you can watch yourself
forget. Aim at the wrong bud and the light is wasted - and what you waste is
yourself. Colour all seven chapters and the rainbow returns to the sky. How
much of you is still there when it does decides which of three endings you
reach: The Way Home, Half a Name, or The White Horse.

HOW TO PLAY
- Move: WASD / arrow keys, or hold and drag anywhere with mouse or touch
  (a virtual stick appears on touch screens).
- Aim: your horn points where you move. Keep holding a direction and the beam
  keeps shining that way - walk in small circles to sweep the light over a bud.
- Choose a colour: press 1-7, tap the colour dots at the bottom, or use Q / E
  to cycle. Each chapter starts with the colour you need already selected.
- A bud opens when it has soaked up enough of its own colour; the ring around
  it shows the progress. Not enough memory left and the light goes dim.
- Advance the story: Space / Enter / click / tap.
- Progress saves automatically. On the title screen press C to continue from
  your last chapter.

Pure Canvas 2D and WebAudio - no images, no sound files, no libraries.
Everything you see and hear is generated in code. Zip: 12770 bytes.
